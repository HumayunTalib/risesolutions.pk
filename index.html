Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get in touch.

How much does a solar system cost in Pakistan?

Cost depends on system size, type (on-grid, hybrid, or off-grid), equipment brands, and whether batteries are included. Share your monthly bill and we will send a tailored quotation.

Which solar system is best for my home?

It depends on your electricity usage, roof space, and how much backup you need during outages. A site assessment gives a clear answer.

What is the difference between on-grid and hybrid systems?

On-grid systems work with the utility grid and have no batteries. Hybrid systems add battery storage, so you keep power during outages and can use stored solar energy after sunset.

Can solar power run an entire factory?

Industrial solar can offset a large share of a factory’s power use, and sizing depends on load, shift pattern, and roof area. We assess your consumption to recommend the right capacity.
